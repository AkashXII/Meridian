**Comprehensive Coverage.** This covers physical damage to your insured motor vehicle from non-collision events while parked or driven, including fire to the vehicle, theft of the vehicle, vandalism to the vehicle, a tree branch or falling object striking the vehicle, and the vehicle striking an animal, up to $10,000 per incident. A $500 deductible applies to each comprehensive vehicle claim.

**Collision Coverage.** Damage to your vehicle resulting from a collision with another vehicle or object is covered up to the vehicle's actual cash value, subject to a $750 collision deductible, which is higher than the comprehensive deductible.

**Liability Coverage.** If you are found at fault in an accident, this policy covers injury or property damage caused to third parties up to $50,000, separate from and in addition to comprehensive or collision coverage.

**Uninsured and Underinsured Motorist Coverage.** If you are struck by a driver who carries no insurance, damage is covered up to $25,000. If the at-fault driver's insurance is insufficient to cover the full loss, this policy covers the shortfall up to the same limit after the other driver's coverage is exhausted.

**Personal Injury Protection.** Medical expenses for the driver and passengers of the insured vehicle are covered up to $10,000 per person, regardless of who was at fault in the accident.

**Roadside Assistance.** Towing, lockout service, and emergency fuel delivery are covered up to $100 per incident, limited to three uses per policy year. Costs beyond the towing distance to the nearest qualified repair facility are the policyholder's responsibility.

**Rental Reimbursement.** While a covered vehicle is undergoing repair following an approved claim, rental car costs are reimbursed up to $30 per day for a maximum of fifteen days.

**Windshield and Glass Coverage.** Chipped or cracked glass that can be repaired rather than replaced is covered with no deductible. Full windshield replacement is subject to the standard comprehensive deductible.

**Total Loss Determination.** A vehicle is declared a total loss when the estimated cost of repair exceeds seventy-five percent of its current market value. Settlement is based on actual cash value, including depreciation, not the original purchase price.

**Exclusions.** This policy does not cover damage sustained while the vehicle is used for organized racing, unlicensed commercial delivery without a rideshare endorsement, or operation by a driver without a valid license. Mechanical or electrical breakdown from normal wear is also excluded; only sudden, accidental damage is covered.

**Unlicensed and Underage Driver Provisions.** Damage occurring while the vehicle is operated by any driver without a valid license, including an underage household member, is excluded from all coverage regardless of the circumstances of the accident. A newly licensed driver under 21 must be disclosed to the insurer within thirty days of obtaining a license.

**Vandalism and Civil Disturbance.** Intentional damage to the vehicle by an individual, including keying, slashed tires, or broken windows, is covered under comprehensive coverage. Damage sustained during a riot, civil commotion, or organized public protest is also covered under comprehensive coverage, subject to the same deductible.
