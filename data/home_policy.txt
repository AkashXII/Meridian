**Dwelling Coverage.** This policy covers damage to the primary dwelling structure from fire, windstorm, theft, vandalism, and sudden accidental water damage such as a burst pipe, subject to a standard $1,000 deductible.

**Flood Exclusion.** Damage caused by flooding, including rising groundwater or water entering the home from outside during heavy rain, is not covered under this policy and requires a separate flood insurance policy.

**Sewer and Drain Backup.** Water entering the home through a backed-up sewer line or drain is excluded from standard coverage unless a sewer backup endorsement has been added, and is treated as distinct from both flood damage and burst pipe damage.

**Personal Property Coverage.** Personal belongings inside the home are covered up to $15,000 combined for theft or covered damage. Jewelry and watches are limited to $2,000 unless individually scheduled. Electronics are limited to $5,000. Firearms are limited to $2,500 and require serial number documentation. Cash and currency are limited to $200 per claim.

**Off-Premises Personal Property.** Personal belongings such as bicycles, luggage, and sports equipment are covered while away from the home up to $1,000 combined, under the same terms as property inside the home. Mechanical wear or failure, such as worn brakes on a bicycle, is not a covered loss.

**Liability Coverage.** If a visitor is injured on your property, this policy covers your legal liability up to $100,000. Medical expenses for an injured guest are covered up to $5,000 separately, regardless of fault.

**Additional Living Expenses.** If the home becomes uninhabitable due to a covered loss, the cost of temporary housing is covered up to $200 per day for a maximum of thirty days.

**Detached Structures.** Garages, sheds, fences, and other structures not attached to the main dwelling are covered up to ten percent of the dwelling coverage limit.

**Earthquake and Earth Movement Exclusion.** Damage from earthquake, landslide, sinkhole collapse, or other earth movement is not covered under a standard policy and requires a separate endorsement.

**General Medical and Injury Coverage.** Where this policy provides liability or medical payments coverage for an injury occurring on the insured property, coverage extends to reasonable and necessary treatment for the injury, including physician visits, emergency care, and follow-up treatment, regardless of the specific nature of the injury.

**Vacancy Provision.** A home left unoccupied for more than sixty consecutive days loses coverage for theft and vandalism claims until the policyholder resumes occupancy or notifies the insurer.

**Mold Remediation.** Mold removal is covered up to $5,000, but only when the mold results directly from a covered water damage event; mold from ongoing humidity or lack of maintenance is excluded.
